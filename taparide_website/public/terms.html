1. The service

Tap A Ride is a technology platform that lets you find, book and pay for rides in Abuja: seats on buses, event buses, shared Tagalong rides and electric cars. The rides themselves are provided by verified transport operators, fleet owners and drivers ("partners"). Tap A Ride checks partners and their vehicles before they can take bookings, but does not itself drive the vehicles.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 16 to open an account. Riders under 18 should travel with a parent's permission.
  • Give true details (name, phone, email) and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • One account per person. We may suspend accounts used for fraud, abuse or to collect offers more than once.

3. The sign-up ride reward

  • When you join the waitlist on taparide.com and agree to these terms, we grant you a sign-up ride reward. Your account shows "Ride reward granted" when you open Tap A Ride with the same email address and confirm it.
  • Until we fully launch, the reward has no set value and cannot be spent. We will announce its value and how to use it at full launch, and tell you by email before it applies.
  • It is for the first 5,000 people who join, one per person, phone number and email address.
  • The reward can only ever be used for rides in the app. It has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or transferred.
  • We may withdraw the reward if it was obtained with fake or duplicate details, automated sign-ups or abuse of invite links.
  • Your place in line and invite count are estimates and may change as people join. They do not guarantee a launch date or any extra benefit.

4. Abuja Tech Week buses (24–26 November 2026)

  • Tap A Ride is the official ride partner of Abuja Tech Week 2026. The buses are free and powered by Pi-CNG & EV.
  • Each person gets one free pass, valid on every event day they pick, from one of the listed pick-up points. One pass per person and per WhatsApp number; duplicate sign-ups may be removed. A pass is not transferable.
  • Buses leave every pick-up point at 7:00 AM sharp. Be there by 7:00 AM; buses do not wait for late riders.
  • Seats are limited. If a bus is full, we will try to place you on the next available bus, but cannot guarantee it.
  • Bring a phone or ID with the name you registered. Our staff may check names against the boarding list.
  • Times and pick-up points may change for safety, traffic or event reasons. We will tell you on WhatsApp or email.

5. Payments and refunds

  • Fares are shown before you book and are set by the operator. You pay from your Tap A Ride wallet, by card, bank transfer or a tap/QR payment.
  • If you cancel before departure, the fare goes back to your wallet. If a partner cancels, you get a full refund to your wallet.
  • Wallet top-ups are handled by our licensed payment providers. We never store your full card details.

6. Safety and conduct

  • Check the plate number on your ticket before you get in. If it doesn't match, don't enter and report it in the app.
  • Treat drivers, staff and other riders with respect. No violence, harassment, weapons, illegal items or damage to vehicles.
  • Wear a seatbelt where one is fitted. Follow the driver's safety instructions.
  • In an emergency call 112. Use "Share my ride" so someone you trust can follow your trip.
