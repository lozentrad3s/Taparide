Questions

How do I get my free ride?

Join the waitlist, then open your Tap A Ride account with the same email and confirm it. ₦1,000 of ride credit lands in your wallet once. It's for the first 5,000 people who join.

Are the Abuja Tech Week buses really free?

Yes. Tap A Ride is the official ride partner of Abuja Tech Week 2026, and the buses, powered by Pi-CNG & EV, cost nothing. Reserve a seat for each day you're going.

Where and when do the Tech Week buses leave?

From six pick-up points: University of Abuja (Gwagwalada), Nnamdi Azikiwe Airport, Old Parade Ground (for corps members), Nile University, Police Signboard in Lugbe, and the checkpoint between Maraba and Nyanya. Every bus leaves at 7:00 AM sharp on 24, 25 and 26 November, so be there by 7.

How do I contact Tap A Ride?

Chat with us on WhatsApp at +234 911 050 2561, or email info@taparide.com. For partnerships and advertising, email partnership@taparide.com.

Who checks the drivers and vehicles?

Every operator, driver and vehicle is checked against their documents before they can take bookings. Your ticket shows the plate number so you can match it before you get in.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to save your place, confirm your seat and tell you when your ride is ready. We don't sell them. Read the privacy notice.
