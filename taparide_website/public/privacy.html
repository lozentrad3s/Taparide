Privacy notice

Last updated 23 November 2026. This notice covers the Tap A Ride website. The Tap A Ride app has its own terms when you open an account.

What we collect

  • When you join the waitlist: your email, and if you give them, your name, phone number, the area you live in and how you want to use Tap A Ride.
  • When you reserve an event-bus seat: the event, day, pick-up point and departure time you pick, and your full name, WhatsApp number and email. One pass covers every day you pick. Your name and a unique pass ID are printed on your personal bus pass and on the boarding list, and the pass is emailed to you. One WhatsApp number can hold one pass, so we check new requests against existing ones to stop duplicates.
  • When someone invites you: which invite link you used, so the person who invited you moves up the list.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider records your IP address briefly to stop abuse (for example, too many sign-ups from one place). We don't use advertising trackers on this website.

Why we use it

  • To save your place on the waitlist and your sign-up ride reward, and to show the reward in your account when you open it with the same email.
  • To plan and confirm event buses, and to tell you the fare, pickup point and time.
  • To email you what you signed up for: a welcome message with your invite link when you join, and your bus pass when you reserve a seat. We send these through our email provider, Resend.
  • To tell you when Tap A Ride launches in your area. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To see, in totals only, which areas need buses most. Bus operators see seat counts by area and time, never your name or contact details, until your seat is confirmed and they need your name to board you.

We process your details because you asked us to (your consent when you join) and to provide the service you signed up for, as allowed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your details. We use trusted service providers to run the website, store data and send our emails (our hosting, database and email providers), under contracts that require them to protect it. Some of these providers may store data outside Nigeria, with safeguards in place.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until you open an account or ask us to delete them, and no longer than 24 months after launch if you never open an account. Event-bus requests are deleted 6 months after the event.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, withdraw your consent, or object to how we use them. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Contact: info@taparide.com or WhatsApp +234 911 050 2561. Partnerships: partnership@taparide.com.

Cookies and storage on your device

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share what your browser stores.

Essential (always on). So the things you asked for are still there when you come back, this website keeps on your device: your waitlist sign-up details and invite code, the private key that lets you reserve seats, your free bus pass, and your cookie choice. The Tap A Ride app keeps your sign-in session on your device so you stay signed in. If we switch on the anti-bot check on our forms, Cloudflare Turnstile may set a short-lived security cookie.

Statistics (only if you allow). Vercel Web Analytics counts visits and pages anonymously, to see what helps people. It sets no cookies and doesn't identify you or follow you to other sites. It stays off unless you choose “Allow statistics”.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. That also removes your saved bus pass from that device; your seats stay booked, the pass is still in your email, and joining again with the same email brings it back.
