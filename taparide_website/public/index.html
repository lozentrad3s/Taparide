Questions

How do I get my free ride?

Join the waitlist on this page, then create your Tap A Ride account with the same email and confirm it. ₦1,000 of ride credit lands in your wallet once, and you can use it on any city bus or event-bus seat. It is for the first 5,000 people to join.

Is the Abuja Tech Week bus free?

Reserving a seat is free and you pay nothing until your bus is confirmed. We then send you the fare, and your free ride covers up to ₦1,000 of it. If your bus doesn't fill, nobody is charged.

When does a bus get confirmed?

When 14 people from the same area pick the same day and departure time. The more people you invite from your area, the sooner it happens. We confirm by email, and on WhatsApp if you gave us your number.

Who checks the drivers and vehicles?

Every operator, driver and vehicle is checked against their documents before they can take bookings on Tap A Ride. Your ticket shows the plate number so you can match it before you get in.

Which areas do you cover?

We are starting with routes across the FCT, including Kubwa, Gwarinpa, Nyanya, Lugbe, Wuse, Maitama, Jabi and the Central Area. Tell us where you live when you join: it decides where buses run next.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to save your place, confirm your seat and tell you when your ride is ready. We don't sell them. Read the privacy notice.
