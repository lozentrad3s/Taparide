# Who runs buses on Tap A Ride's corridors

Research date: 18 November 2026. This is **public information** about who runs vehicles on each corridor the app lists. None of these operators are Tap A Ride partners and no agreements exist with any of them. Bookings on Tap A Ride are only with verified partners.

The operators attached to routes in the demo data ("Abuja Metro Bus Co.", "Capital Metro Rail Services", "Kubwa Express Shuttles" and the rest) are **fictional samples**. They are labelled "(sample)" in `lib/core/backend/demo_seed.dart`, `supabase/seed.sql` and, for the live database, migration `20261118020000_route_operators.sql`.

Where the data lives:

- App: `lib/core/route_operators.dart` (`corridorOperators`, attached to each `TransitRoute.servedBy`), shown on the route details page under "Who runs buses on this corridor today".
- Database: `public.routes.served_by` (jsonb), added and filled by `supabase/migrations/20261118020000_route_operators.sql` and set again in `supabase/seed.sql`.

Each entry has a name, a type (CNG bus, diesel bus, light rail and so on), a note, the source link, the publisher and the month it was reported. Entries flagged `uncertain` come from older reports or don't confirm the exact corridor in 2026; the app shows them as "Not confirmed for 2026".

## What we found across Abuja

- **Presidential Initiative on CNG and EV (Pi-CNG & EV, formerly P-CNGi).** In November 2024 the federal government handed 15 high-capacity CNG buses to three transport unions: NURTW, RTEAN and NARTO ([ThisDay, Nov 2024](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2024/11/29/fg-hands-over-15-high-capacity-cng-buses-to-leadership-of-operating-unions); [Nairametrics, Nov 2024](https://nairametrics.com/2024/11/27/pcngi-transport-unions-sign-agreement-for-discounted-hybrid-cng-bus-routes-in-abuja/)). On 1 October 2026 Pi-CNG & EV said 13 buses are in commercial service in the FCT, with 26 more expected, and quoted CNG and diesel fares on four FCT routes: Kubwa–Berger, Apo–Nyanya/Mararaba, Masaka–Berger and Berger–Gwagwalada ([NAN, Oct 2026](https://nannews.ng/october-1-cng-ev-interventions-reduce-transport-fares-across-states-pi-cng-ev/); [Legit.ng, Oct 2026](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/industry/1734144-fg-reports-transport-fares-fct-11-states-lists-cheaper-routes/)). No source says which union runs which route.
- **NURTW at Area 1 park (Garki branch).** In May 2025 P-CNGi and NURTW enforced a 40% fare cut on CNG-powered commercial vehicles from Area 1 to Gwagwalada, Bwari/Dutse, Kuje, Galadimawa, Mpape, Zuba, Lugbe, Jabi, Wuse, Nyanya, Kabusa and Apo ([Nairametrics, May 2025](https://nairametrics.com/2025/05/02/p-cngi-enforces-40-fare-reduction-on-cng-commercial-vehicles-in-abuja/)). The report doesn't say what kind of vehicle (bus, minibus or taxi), and nothing since confirms these fares still apply, so the app marks these entries uncertain.
- **Informal sector.** Most trips are still in commercial buses, Coaster buses and taxis whose operators aren't named; fares are often bargained at the roadside. Daily Trust's March 2026 fare survey (carried by Trust Radio) is the main source for corridor fares ([Trust Radio, 16 Mar 2026](https://trustradio.com.ng/en/fuel-hike-how-abuja-residents-spend-minutes-bargaining-for-affordable-rides/); [Trust Radio, 17 Mar 2026](https://trustradio.com.ng/en/abuja-residents-negotiate-transport-fares-as-fuel-price-soar)). A Legit.ng survey on 3 October 2026 found no noticeable fare relief on some FCT routes after the 1 October CNG target ([Legit.ng, Oct 2026](https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/industry/1734125-operators-release-fares-states-fgs-promises-transport-cuts-passengers-react/)).
- **Abuja Light Rail.** Relaunched 27–29 May 2024; operated by CCECC ([Nairametrics, Sep 2024](https://nairametrics.com/2024/09/20/abuja-light-rail-moves-over-250000-passengers-in-100-days)). In August 2026 Daily Trust described the FCTA-run Yellow Line from Abuja Metro Station to Nnamdi Azikiwe Airport (Kukwaba 1 and 2, Wupa, Idu, Bassanjiwa), about 20 minutes, four trips a day at 08:00, 10:00, 15:30 and 17:30 ([Daily Trust, Aug 2026](https://dailytrust.com/?p=1937118)). We found no 2025–2026 source with current fares.
- **AUMTCO (Abuja Urban Mass Transport Company).** Historically ran Abuja–Mararaba, –Bwari, –Kuje, –Gwagwalada, –Suleja and city routes. In August 2023 the FCTA said it would revive it, and reported that many buses were out of use at its Kubwa head office ([ICIR, Aug 2023](https://www.icirnigeria.org/fcta-to-revive-mass-transport-buses-introduce-bi-weekly-sanitation/)). We found **no 2025–2026 report** of AUMTCO buses running, so it isn't listed in the app.
- **FCTA buses.** In February 2024 the FCTA launched the private TFA 360 Transportation System with 14 air-conditioned buses; no routes were published ([Nairametrics, Feb 2024](https://nairametrics.com/2024/02/27/fcta-and-private-sector-unveil-affordable-mass-transit-buses-for-abuja-residents)). Around August 2025 the FCTA Transport Secretariat received 4 CNG hybrid buses from P-CNGi, no routes given ([Leadership](https://leadership.ng/fcta-receives-cng-hybrid-buses-from-presidential-initiative-to-boost-public-transport/)). Not listed in the app because no corridor is confirmed.
- **Electric buses.** On 26 August 2026 the federal government commissioned the first 37 of 100 electric buses for federal civil servants. They run a pilot on unnamed routes linking residential corridors to the Federal Secretariat, managed by an unnamed special-purpose company ([WithinNigeria, Aug 2026](https://www.withinnigeria.com/2026/08/27/fg-rolls-out-first-37-of-100-electric-buses-for-civil-servants-routes-benefits-and-what-happens-next/)). These buses are for staff only and their routes aren't published, so they aren't listed. An older CNG staff-bus pilot (2022) listed Berger/Kado/Gwagwa/Karmo, Kubwa/Dutse Alhaji and AYA/Nyanya/Mararaba ([Leadership](https://leadership.ng/federal-govt-launches-gas-powered-buses-for-civil-servants/)); its current status is unknown.

## Caveats

- Fares move quickly with fuel prices, and the sources sometimes disagree. One commuter quoted ₦2,500 for Nyanya Bridge–Zuba, for example. Treat fares as indicative.
- The app splits the rail service into a "Metro Line" (Metro Station–Idu) and an "Airport Line" (Idu–Airport). The 2026 reports describe a single Yellow Line from Abuja Metro Station to the airport, plus a Blue Line from Idu to Kubwa (Gbazango) in the network plan. Its 2026 service level isn't confirmed.
- We could not confirm which union (NURTW, RTEAN or NARTO) runs each Pi-CNG & EV route.
- Several Gazette and Leadership pages carry 2026 dates in their headers but are older articles. We used only the article dates.
