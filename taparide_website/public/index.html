Questions

How do I get my free ride?

Join the waitlist, then open your Tap A Ride account with the same email and confirm it. ₦1,000 of ride credit lands in your wallet once. It's for the first 5,000 people who join.

Are the Abuja Tech Week buses really free?

Yes. Tap A Ride is the official ride partner of Abuja Tech Week 2026, and the event buses cost nothing. Reserve a seat for each day you're going.

When is my Tech Week bus confirmed?

When 14 people from the same area pick the same day and time. Invite colleagues from your area to fill it faster. We confirm by email, and on WhatsApp if you gave your number.

Who checks the drivers and vehicles?

Every operator, driver and vehicle is checked against their documents before they can take bookings. Your ticket shows the plate number so you can match it before you get in.

What do you do with my details?

We use them to save your place, confirm your seat and tell you when your ride is ready. We don't sell them. Read the privacy notice.
